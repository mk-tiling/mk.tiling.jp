Modulo Krinkle

Research · 2025–ongoing

Modulo Krinkle is a family of non-periodic tilings derived from modular arithmetic and vector geometry.

The construction produces global order from simple integer sequences and placement rules.

Explore interactively

Explore MK’s spiral tiling system on your device. Adjust the parameters to see how the pattern changes. On larger screens, you can also export the tile outlines as a DXF file.

Open the Playground →

Research development

The initial construction was published as an independently submitted arXiv preprint in June 2025.

The later paper Spiral Tilings Derived from Modular Arithmetic develops a unified approach to spiral tilings made from congruent tiles. MK presented it at Bridges 2026 in Galway on 5 August 2026.
