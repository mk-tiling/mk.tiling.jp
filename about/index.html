MK

Mathematician / Artist

MK is an independent researcher and artist. MK Tiling grew out of MK’s study of mathematical tilings and continues that exploration through research and art.

Practice

MK explores mathematical structures through research papers, digital art, interactive works, and physical objects. Across these formats, the practice asks how mathematical structures can be understood not only through formal description, but also through observation, interaction, and physical experience.

Current research

Modulo Krinkle

Modulo Krinkle is a family of non-periodic tilings derived from modular arithmetic and vector geometry. The first preprint was posted to arXiv in 2025. A later development, Spiral Tilings Derived from Modular Arithmetic, was presented at Bridges Galway 2026.

  • Learn more about Modulo Krinkle
  • Open Playground

Seabed Tiling

Seabed is a tiling generated by a recognizable substitution on six types of marked rhombs, with inflation factor $\varphi^3$. Its tiling space is mutually locally derivable (MLD) with the Anti-Penrose pentagrid hull.

Community

MK runs the Facebook group DefinitelyTiled.

A group for sharing well-defined tilings and tessellations — especially those that are mathematically describable and spark curiosity.

We welcome educational and informative posts that help us understand how and why a tiling or space-filling structure works.

Since our minds tend to find patterns in almost anything, this group focuses on actual, well-defined structures.
